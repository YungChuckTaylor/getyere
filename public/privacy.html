Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version: Yere uses your information to provide reminders, account sync, trusted-contact sharing, attachments, and notifications. We do not sell personal information, run third-party advertising, or use reminder content to train artificial-intelligence models.

Information we process

  • Account information: email address, display name, user ID, authentication records, theme, time zone, and preferences.
  • Content you choose to add: reminder titles, notes, dates, links, recurrence settings, lists, completion history, attachments, and trusted-contact details.
  • Notification information: browser push subscriptions or native device tokens and delivery status. We use these only to deliver notifications you request.
  • Security and service records: invite, audit, rate-limit, and error records needed to protect accounts and operate the service.
  • Voice capture: microphone access begins only after you choose voice capture. Speech is converted into reminder text; Yere does not intentionally store the raw audio recording. Your device or browser speech-recognition provider may process audio under its own terms.

Why we use it

We process information to authenticate you, sync your workspace, schedule and deliver reminders, support trusted-contact features, secure invitations, provide private file storage, troubleshoot failures, comply with law, and respond to support requests. Pattern insights are based on completion timing and are calculated on your device.

Service providers and sharing

Yere uses service providers acting on our instructions, including Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; Vercel for the website; Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service for native push; browser push services for Web Push; and configured email or SMS delivery providers. Trusted contacts see only information needed for invitations and reminders you permit. We may disclose information when legally required or needed to protect users and the service.

Storage, security, and retention

Application tables use row-level access controls. Attachments are held in a private, user-scoped bucket. Secret invitation values are hashed, limited, single-use, and expire. Offline copies may remain in your browser or the app’s private device storage so Yere can work without a connection. We keep account data while your account is active and for only as long afterward as required for security, backup rotation, disputes, or law.

Your choices and rights

  • Change notification permission in Yere or device settings.
  • Edit or delete reminders, contacts, and attachments in the app.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Account & data → Delete account and cloud data. This permanently removes the account and associated cloud data, subject to short-lived backups and legal obligations.
  • Request access, correction, export, objection, or other rights available under applicable law by contacting us.

Children

Yere is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied information should contact us.

International processing

Providers may process information in countries other than yours. We use provider safeguards and access controls appropriate to the service and applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act where applicable.

Changes and contact

We will update the date above when this policy materially changes. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@getyere.com or through the Yere support page.
