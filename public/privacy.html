1. Who we are and what this covers

Yere (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is the operator of the reminder and trusted-contact service at getyere.com and through its web and mobile apps. This policy explains our handling of personal information under applicable Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies, and applicable provincial or territorial privacy law.

Alberta and British Columbia have private-sector Personal Information Protection Acts. Québec has the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended by Law 25. Which law applies depends on the activity and jurisdiction; PIPEDA may apply to interprovincial or international commercial information flows even where a provincial law also applies. This policy does not take away protections that apply to you elsewhere.

Privacy contact: direct requests or complaints to the person responsible for privacy at Yere through support@getyere.com, using the subject “Privacy request”, or through Yere support. Do not include passwords or sensitive documents in your first message.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: your email address, display name, account identifier, authentication records, and preferences such as theme and time zone. When you use Google sign-in, we receive the identity information Google makes available for the permissions you approve, such as your email address, name, and profile image. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages. Apple sign-in, where available, supplies the identity information you authorise Apple to share.
  • Your workspace: reminders, notes, due dates, recurrence rules, links, lists, completion history, optional costs, and files you upload.
  • Trusted-contact information: names and email addresses you provide, invitations, relationship permissions, delegated reminders, and block or revocation records. Only provide another person’s information when you have an appropriate reason and permission to do so.
  • Device and delivery information: notification permissions, push subscription endpoints or device tokens, platform information, and delivery records.
  • Support and security records: messages you send us, responses, service errors, audit events, and technical information such as IP addresses or request metadata processed by our hosting and authentication providers.

3. Purposes and meaningful consent

We use information to create and secure accounts; sync and restore your workspace; schedule and deliver reminders; manage trusted-contact permissions; store attachments; provide support; investigate misuse and failures; and meet legal obligations. Completion-based insights are calculated on your device. We limit collection, use and disclosure to information reasonably necessary for identified, appropriate purposes.

We seek meaningful consent for collection, use and disclosure where Canadian law requires it, explaining the information involved, purposes, relevant recipients and reasonably foreseeable consequences. Express consent is required where the law requires it, including for sensitive information in applicable circumstances. We may act without consent only when permitted or required by applicable law; “legitimate interests” is not a general substitute for the consent Canadian law requires.

Accepting the Terms of Use or reading this policy is not blanket consent to every use of your information. We will provide notice and obtain any consent required before using information for a materially new purpose. You may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice, by contacting our privacy contact. We will explain the consequences: withdrawing consent needed for a feature may mean we cannot provide that feature, but unnecessary collection is not a condition of service.

4. Microphone and voice input

Voice capture starts only after you press the microphone control and grant any required permission. Yere uses the resulting transcript to help create a reminder. Yere does not record, retain, or upload raw microphone audio itself. Your browser, operating system, or speech-recognition provider may send audio to its own servers to recognise speech, under its own privacy terms. The transcript becomes workspace content if you save it. You can use typed input instead and revoke microphone or speech permissions in device settings.

5. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information, use reminder content to train AI models, or run third-party advertising in the app.

  • Trusted contacts: people involved in an invitation or shared reminder can see the identity, reminder content, and relationship information needed for that feature, according to the permissions you set. Revoking access does not erase copies someone has already received.
  • Service providers: Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage, and server functions; Vercel hosts the website. Google processes Google sign-in when you choose it. Browser push services, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service, and configured email or SMS providers process delivery information when those channels are enabled and used. Device/browser speech providers may process voice input as described above.
  • Authorised personnel: support and administration staff may access information needed to resolve requests, operate the service, or investigate security issues, subject to access controls.
  • Legal and business reasons: we may disclose information when required by law, to protect people or the service, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

Notification previews, emails, and shared reminders can reveal their contents to people with access to the receiving device or inbox. Avoid putting highly sensitive information in notification text.

6. Cookies, local storage, and offline copies

Yere uses browser storage, app-private storage, cached app files, and authentication tokens to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and support offline use and reconnect syncing. The administrative console also uses a secure session cookie. These are functional mechanisms, not advertising trackers.

Offline workspace copies may remain on a device. Signing out is not a substitute for clearing browser site data or removing app data from a shared device. Clearing local data before pending changes sync can permanently lose those changes.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain workspace information while needed to provide your account and the features you use. You can delete content in the app and request account deletion through Settings → Account & data → Delete account and cloud data. If you cannot sign in, contact support from the account email address; we may need to verify ownership.

Account deletion is designed to remove your account and associated active cloud workspace data, including attachments and subscriptions. Some security, audit, support, legal, or dispute records may need to be retained for their relevant purpose. Backup copies may remain until the applicable backup lifecycle completes. Copies received by trusted contacts and data stored on other devices are not necessarily removed by a cloud deletion request. We do not promise an immediate purge from every backup or third-party system.

8. Security, incidents and processing outside Canada

We use authentication, access controls, row-level database policies and private file storage to help protect information, with safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity. No service or transmission method is completely secure; secure your devices and never share sign-in codes.

Our hosting and service providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States depending on provider configuration. We do not promise Canada-only hosting. Information processed abroad may be subject to lawful access by courts, law enforcement or other authorities in that jurisdiction.

We remain accountable for personal information transferred to service providers for processing and use contractual and other safeguards as required by applicable law. Where Québec law applies, its privacy impact assessment and written-agreement requirements must be met before a covered transfer outside Québec. Contact our privacy contact for information about our processing arrangements; this policy is not a claim that every provider is located in Canada.

If a privacy incident occurs, applicable Canadian breach-assessment, recordkeeping, reporting and notification duties apply. Where PIPEDA applies, breaches creating a real risk of significant harm must be reported to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and affected individuals notified as soon as feasible after that determination. Other applicable provincial notification requirements also apply.

9. Access, correction and privacy complaints

Subject to applicable law and its exceptions, you may request access to personal information we hold about you, an explanation of its use and disclosure, and correction of inaccurate or incomplete information. You may also withdraw consent and request deletion, subject to lawful retention and other restrictions. PIPEDA does not create an unconditional right to immediate erasure of all records. Where applicable provincial law provides additional rights, such as a portable copy of qualifying computerized information in Québec, you may request those rights through our privacy contact.

  • Edit or delete workspace content and manage trusted contacts in Yere.
  • Manage notification, microphone and speech permissions in the app, browser or device settings.
  • Disconnect Google access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. This does not itself delete your Yere account.
  • Email support@getyere.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may take proportionate steps to verify your identity and protect other people’s information. We will explain any legally permitted refusal, fee or extension and the available complaint route.

Where PIPEDA applies, we respond to access requests within 30 calendar days, unless a lawful extension applies, with the required notice. Access is provided at minimal or no cost as required by law. Where provincial law sets another applicable deadline or process, we follow that requirement.

You can raise a privacy concern with us and complain to the relevant regulator without giving up your legal rights: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or Québec’s Commission d’accès à l’information, as applicable. Contacting us first is not a mandatory waiver or barrier to a regulator complaint.

10. Children and young users

Yere is not intended for children under 13. In Québec, children under 14 should not create an account or provide personal information through Yere. A higher minimum age or parental/guardian involvement may be required by the laws that apply to a young user. We do not treat a young person’s consent as valid where they cannot understand the nature, purposes and consequences of the processing.

We do not knowingly seek information from children below these service age limits. If you believe a child has provided information contrary to these limits, contact our privacy contact so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes and provide additional notice and obtain any consent required for material changes or new purposes. Changes do not remove rights you have under applicable Canadian law. The current policy is available at getyere.com/privacy-policy.

12. Electronic messages and marketing choices

Creating a Yere account, allowing push notifications or accepting a trusted contact does not by itself constitute express consent to marketing. If we send commercial electronic messages covered by Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), we must have the required consent or a lawful exception, identify the sender with required contact information, and provide a working unsubscribe mechanism where required. Applicable unsubscribe requests must be honoured without delay and no later than 10 business days.

You can contact support about messaging choices. Service and security communications are assessed under the applicable rules, not automatically treated as exempt merely because they are labelled “transactional”. Do not use Yere invitations or shared reminders to send unsolicited commercial messages.
