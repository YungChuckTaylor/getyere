How can we help?

Support for the Yere web, Android, and iOS apps.

Contact support
Email support@getyere.com. Include your device type and app version, but never send your password, one-time code, private key, or full notification token.

Notifications are not arriving

  1. Open Yere → Settings and turn on push.
  2. Confirm notifications are allowed in your phone or browser settings.
  3. Create a reminder a few minutes ahead and choose Push or Both.
  4. Use “Send a test notification” in Settings.
  5. On the web, install Yere first where the browser requires it. On iPhone Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Offline changes

The installed app keeps a private local snapshot and queues cloud changes while disconnected. Reopen Yere with a connection and leave it open briefly to sync. If a change still does not appear, sign out only after confirming the device is online.

Sign-in or account recovery

Check that you are using the same email address and that your confirmation or recovery link has not expired. Request a new recovery email from the sign-in screen if needed. Yere support will never ask for your password.

Delete your account

While signed in, go to Settings → Account & data → Delete account and cloud data. Confirm the warning and type DELETE. This permanently removes the account and associated reminders, contacts, attachments, subscriptions, and settings. If you cannot access the app, email support from the account address.

Privacy and safety

Review the Privacy Policy. You can revoke a trusted contact or block that account from the Trusted contacts screen. For an urgent safety issue, use the subject “Urgent safety report” when contacting support.
